Artist Statement

I grew up playing video games, especially Role-Playing Games (RPGs). One franchise that has had a strong influence on me is The Elder Scrolls. The fantasy visuals first caught my attention; however, the soundtrack and ambient sounds were what truly transported me into another world, far away from reality. I especially enjoyed the nature and exploration of these games. Exploring forests, mountains, ruins, and other environments makes the world feel alive and gives me a sense of discovery. These experiences have made me interested in creating game environments that can give players that same exact feeling of immersion and adventure. My main goal is to create game environments that are beautiful, imaginative, and immersive. Therefore, I want to develop environments that make players feel curious about their surroundings and encourage them to explore, while also giving them a chance to temporarily escape from reality. I currently have experience with drawing and sketching, and I also have some programming experience using Python. Throughout this course, I want to improve my creativity and develop stronger ideas for my environments. I also want to improve my drawing skills and learn how to create environments that feel more detailed and alive. Ultimately, I hope to combine my interests in art, exploration, and game design to create worlds that allow people to escape reality for a while and experience something new.
